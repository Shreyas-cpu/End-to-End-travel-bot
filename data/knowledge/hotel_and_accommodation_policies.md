# Hotel Accommodation Policies & Taxes

## Check-In & Check-Out Times
- Standard Check-In: 15:00 (3:00 PM) local time. Early check-in subject to room availability upon arrival.
- Standard Check-Out: 11:00 AM local time. Late check-out can be requested via front desk.

## City Tourist Taxes (Taxe de Séjour / City Tax)
- Local government tourist taxes are determined per person, per night (e.g. Paris €2.60 to €5.20, London, Rome). Usually settled at hotel reception.

## Cancellation Policies
- Free cancellation rates allow modifications up to 24-48 hours before the scheduled check-in date.
- Non-refundable rates provide a discount but forfeit payment upon cancellation.
