# International Visa & Passport Regulations

## Passport Validity Requirements
- Most international destinations (including the Schengen Area, UK, Japan, and UAE) require passports to be valid for at least 3 to 6 months beyond the intended departure date.
- Passports must contain at least 2 consecutive blank visa pages for entry stamps.

## Schengen Area (Europe / France)
- Non-EU travelers: Up to 90 days within any 180-day period without a visa for short-stay tourism.
- ETIAS (European Travel Information and Authorization System): Pre-travel online security authorization required for visa-exempt nationals.
- Mandatory travel medical insurance with minimum €30,000 coverage valid across all Schengen member states.

## United Kingdom Entry
- ETA (Electronic Travel Authorisation) required for eligible non-visa nationals for stays up to 6 months for tourism and business.
