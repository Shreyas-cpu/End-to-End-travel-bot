# Airline Baggage Policies & Restrictions

## Carry-On Baggage Guidelines
- Standard allowance for Economy cabin: 1 personal item (fits under seat) + 1 carry-on bag (up to 10 kg / 22 lbs, max dimensions 55 x 40 x 23 cm).
- Business & First Class allowance: Up to 2 carry-on bags (up to 14 kg each) + 1 personal laptop bag.
- Liquids, aerosols, and gels (LAGs): Maximum 100 ml (3.4 oz) per container, placed in a single transparent, resealable plastic quart-sized bag.
- Lithium batteries & power banks: Must remain in carry-on luggage only. Maximum capacity allowed without airline approval is 100Wh (approx 27,000mAh).

## Checked Baggage Guidelines
- Standard Economy: 1 checked bag up to 23 kg (50 lbs) included on transatlantic flights; budget fares may require an add-on.
- Premium Economy: 2 checked bags up to 23 kg each.
- Business & First Class: 2 to 3 checked bags up to 32 kg (70 lbs) each with priority baggage tag handling.
- Excess baggage fees apply for weight exceeding 23 kg or dimensions beyond 158 cm (length + width + height).
